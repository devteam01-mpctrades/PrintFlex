01The service

PrintFlex generates PDF invoices, packing slips and pick lists from your Shopify orders, prints a QR code and barcode on those documents, and provides a scan page where staff can check items off and mark an order packed, which writes a tag back to the order in Shopify.

Features described on our website as planned or "V2" are not part of the service until they ship.

02Plans and the order meter

PrintFlex is offered on three plans:

PlanPriceIncluded orders
Free$050 orders per calendar month
Premium$4.99 per month, or $49.90 per year500 orders per calendar month
Unlimited$9.99 per month, or $99.90 per yearUnlimited

What the meter counts

An order counts once, in the calendar month in which the first document of any type is generated for it. Reprinting or generating further documents for the same order in that month does not count again. Orders for which no document is generated never count. The meter resets on the first day of each calendar month in UTC.

When a Free or Premium store reaches its monthly limit, further document generation is paused until the next month or until the plan is upgraded. Scan mode and reprints of already-generated documents continue to work.

03Billing

All charges are made exclusively through Shopify's Billing API. When you choose a paid plan, Shopify shows you its own charge approval screen; nothing is billed until you approve it there. Charges appear on your Shopify invoice.

We never collect payment details on this website or in the app, and no one from MPC Trades will ask you for card details.

Prices are in US dollars and exclusive of any taxes Shopify may apply. Annual plans are billed once a year in advance.
