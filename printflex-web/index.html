02 — How it works

Five steps, from order list to sealed box

  1. Sort by fulfillment status, tag, date, country or shipping method, then select what you want. Save the views you use every morning so tomorrow is one click.

    • Saved views
    • Multi-select
    • Print status

  2. Up to 250 orders in a single combined file — invoices, packing slips, or both. The job runs in the background with a progress bar, then downloads and opens your printer dialog.

  3. The pick list merges every line across the selected orders into one SKU-sorted sheet. Your picker collects all 34 orders' worth of stock in a single pass instead of thirty-four.

  4. Point a phone camera at the QR code, or fire a USB scanner at the barcode. The order opens instantly with its item checklist, quantities and shipping method.

  5. Tick the items, hit Mark as packed, and PrintFlex writes a tag and a timestamp onto the order. Anyone looking at Shopify admin sees the same truth as the person at the bench.

05 — Scan mode

The part nobody else bundles

Scan mode is an ordinary responsive web page. There is nothing to install on the phone, no Shopify login for warehouse staff, and no separate subscription.

Works with whatever is on the bench

A phone camera reads the QR through the browser's own barcode support. A $30 USB laser scanner types the order number straight into the page like a keyboard. If the camera struggles on an old handset, the scanner still works — the camera is the bonus, not the requirement.

Staff get access, not accounts

The scan page opens from a signed link and a store PIN. Warehouse staff never need a Shopify account, and every scan is logged against the device name so you can see who packed what.

07 — FAQ

Questions merchants actually ask

What counts toward my monthly order limit?
Billing

An order counts once, in the calendar month you first generate a document for it — invoice, packing slip or pick list. Reprinting that order later in the same month is free, and orders you never print never count. The counter resets on the first of each month.

Do I need to buy a barcode scanner?
Hardware

No. Any phone with a camera and a modern browser reads the QR code straight from the page, with nothing to install. If you already have a USB laser scanner on the bench, that works too, because we also print a Code 128 barcode of the order number that any hardware scanner understands.

What exactly is a pick list?
How it works

A pick list takes every line item across the orders you selected, merges the quantities and sorts them by SKU, so you get one sheet for one walk through the warehouse. Instead of visiting the same shelf thirty times for thirty orders, your picker collects the whole batch's stock in a single pass.

Can my invoices look like my brand?
How it works

Yes. Upload your logo, choose an accent colour and a font, pick A4 or Letter, and toggle the fields you want shown, such as prices, taxes, discounts, phone number and HS codes. You see a live preview rendered against one of your real orders, so what you approve is what prints.

What languages and currencies are supported?
How it works

English, French, German, Spanish, Japanese and Korean at launch, chosen per document from the store's language or the customer's country. Amounts print in the presentment currency the customer paid in, with the tax lines exactly as Shopify records them. The translation files are plain text, so the community can extend them.

How does PrintFlex stop double shipping?
Data & safety

Every order in the dashboard shows one of three states: never printed, printed, or packed. The state is written the moment a document is generated, so a second person opening the same batch sees it is already printed before they hit the button. Marking an order packed is a deliberate step on the scan page, not a side effect.

What data can PrintFlex see?
Data & safety

Only the order data needed to print a document: customer name, shipping and billing address, line items, quantities and totals. The app asks Shopify for permission to read orders and to write a tag on them, and nothing more. PrintFlex never sees payment or card data.

What happens to the PDFs you generate?
Data & safety

Generated PDFs are cached so a reprint is instant, then deleted automatically after 30 days. If you need a document after that, it is regenerated on demand from the order. When Shopify sends a redaction request for a customer or a store, we delete the cached documents as well as the database rows.

Do warehouse staff need a Shopify account?
How it works

No. The QR code opens a signed link to that order's pack screen, and staff unlock it with a store PIN you set. Nobody on the bench needs a Shopify login, and every scan is logged against the device name so you can see who packed what.

Is PrintFlex on the Shopify App Store yet?
Billing

Not yet. PrintFlex is in active development and we are onboarding early-access stores directly, which is what the form below is for. Once the app is installed, any paid plan is billed through Shopify's Billing API and approved on Shopify's own charge screen; this website never collects payment.
